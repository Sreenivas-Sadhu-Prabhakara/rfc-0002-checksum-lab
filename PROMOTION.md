## Community post

We are turning the RFC series into small, useful learning tools. This edition explores RFC 2, a legacy Host Software proposal, through Checksum Lab. Compare folded 8-bit checksums after editing, flipping a bit or swapping bytes. Not the full proposed grouped, shifted checksum and not a modern integrity or security tool. Equal sums can hide changes. The archived RFC reports a missing first page.

Try it: https://sreenivas-sadhu-prabhakara.github.io/rfc-0002-checksum-lab/
Source and contributions: https://github.com/Sreenivas-Sadhu-Prabhakara/rfc-0002-checksum-lab
Original document: https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc2.txt

## Teaching exercise

Click “Flip first bit,” then “Copy original,” then “Swap first two bytes.” Compare the two outcomes. Try FF 01: why is the folded result 01 rather than 00?
